---
name: unslop-prose
description: Simplify supplied prose by removing generic AI filler, repetition, and unnatural phrasing while preserving its meaning and register.
---

Remove filler, inflated claims, vague wording, formulaic structure, and repetition that do not improve the message.

1. Preserve the underlying meaning, facts, tone, audience, and language. Do not add claims, conclusions, examples, or details that are not supported by the source.

2. Prefer direct, specific sentences. Remove vague intensifiers, generic transitions, fake headings, repetitive summaries, stock phrases, and chatbot or sycophantic phrasing.

3. Remove vague attributions unless the source is named. Replace abstract praise, feeling-based claims, and generic conclusions with concrete facts, instructions, or evidence. Cut claims that cannot be made concrete.

4. Use plain words and direct constructions. Avoid inflated vocabulary, fancy alternatives to "is" or "has", forced "not just X, but Y" framing, and abstract technical metaphors when a concrete word works.

5. Keep useful structure, but remove sections, lists, or explanations that do not help the reader understand or act. Do not force a rule of three, false ranges, or synonym cycling. Split dense sentences when doing so improves clarity, and prefer active voice when the actor matters.

6. Match the original register unless the request asks for a different one.

7. Avoid mid-sentence semicolons and dash punctuation, including em dashes, en dashes, and hyphens used as dashes. Use a period or comma instead.

8. Return the revised prose first. Explain only changes that affect meaning, facts, or tone.
